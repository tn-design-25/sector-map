Prototype controls
  • Zoom feel. Click a district, then use the arrows in the panel to pan between sectors.
  • Exit paths. Back button, Esc, or click empty ground. All three zoom back out.
  • Pins. Hover a pin or a name in the panel. The building lights up and a card opens.
  • Phone. Tap a district to zoom in. Pins turn into numbers. Tap near one, or tap a market in the panel, to zoom closer. Drag to look around.
